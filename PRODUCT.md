# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Arabic readers on mobile and desktop, including older readers.
## Product Purpose
وِرد is a free noncommercial library created for فتحية إبراهيم الشهري by her child, publishing her writings and legally redistributable old books. Success is finding, reading or downloading a book with minimal steps.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript on existing GitHub Pages. No accounts, ads, subscriptions or tracking. Dedicated home, library, about, book and reader pages. Accessible RTL, light/dark modes, local reading progress, gradual page loading, fullscreen, zoom, optional motion, single/mobile and double/desktop pages.
## Brand Commitments
Name وِرد. Complete replacement of incumbent identity authorized. Contemporary Arabic typography without repeated Islamic decoration. User delegates design decisions and requests direct execution without repeated questions.
## Evidence on Hand
Original 27-page PDF روضة الأنس في أدعية النفس, إعداد وكتابة فتحية إبراهيم الشهري. Publishing explicitly authorized by user. Preserve original PDF and cover proportions. No other books available; do not invent entries.
## Product Principles
Reading comes first. Keep controls simple. Preserve original book content. Mobile and accessibility are essential.
